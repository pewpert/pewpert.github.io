Onward leg

Keep it running for other people

just say when. is the first thing I've built that strangers can use. Shipping it was the easy half. Keeping it reliable is the next thing to learn.

The same question I ask at work applies to my own tools: will people still use this in three months? For the next build I'm focusing on the parts a weekend MVP skips.

  • Security rules and auth reviewed before launch, not after
  • Monitoring, so I hear about a failure before a user does
  • Real-device testing on the phones people actually carry
  • A release habit: small changes, shipped often, easy to roll back
