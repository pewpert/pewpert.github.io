About

From selling the product to shaping it

I started in B2B sales and business development, and led global sales for Kotozna's AI CMS before moving into product management there. Sitting across the table from customers taught me to start from the problem they describe, not the feature they ask for.

Today I own the backlog for the AI CMS, a no-code generative-AI platform for conversational assistants and avatars. I interview business teams, turn what they need into clear requirements, and use Claude Code to test features across different AI models before they reach our developers.

My training is in industrial and systems engineering (MSc, NUS), which is why I reach for numbers early: what does this cost, how often does it happen, what changes if we fix it. The tools on this page come from the same instinct, applied to my own work.
